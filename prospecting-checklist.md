 Prospecting Checklist

Use this checklist to stay consistent, organized, and effective during daily prospecting. These steps help field reps and small teams maximize results with minimal effort.



 Daily Prospecting Checklist

1. Review Today’s Follow‑Ups
 Check your reminders  
 Prioritize hot leads  
 Clear overdue tasks

2. Add New Leads
 Add new businesses or contacts  
 Verify contact details  
 Tag industry and lead source

3. Make Initial Outreach
 Call, visit, or email new leads  
 Log each attempt  
 Add notes for context

4. Engage Active Leads
 Respond to messages  
 Ask discovery questions  
 Move qualified leads to the next stage

5. Update Pipeline Stages
 Move leads forward  
 Mark stalled leads  
 Add follow‑up reminders

6. Send Materials
 Brochures  
 Pricing sheets  
 Product links  
 Demo scheduling links

7. Capture Objections
 Write down concerns  
 Add competitor info  
 Plan your next response

8. Prepare Tomorrow’s Tasks
 Set reminders  
 Organize leads by priority  
 Review upcoming appointments



 Weekly Prospecting Checklist

Review Pipeline Health
 Are leads stuck?  
 Are follow‑ups consistent?  
 Are proposals going out?

Clean Up Data
 Remove duplicates  
 Update contact info  
 Add missing notes

Analyze Wins & Losses
 What’s working?  
 What’s slowing deals?  
 What objections repeat?

Plan Outreach Strategy
 Choose industries to target  
 Identify high‑value areas  
 Prepare scripts or templates



 Monthly Prospecting Checklist

Evaluate Lead Sources
 Which sources produce the best leads?  
 Which sources waste time?

Review Goals
 Deals closed  
 Pipeline value  
 Outreach consistency

Improve Workflow
 Update scripts  
 Adjust pipeline stages  
 Add new templates or resources



Consistent prospecting builds momentum. Use this checklist to stay organized, close more deals, and keep your pipeline full.
