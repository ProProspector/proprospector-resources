
Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?
Your data is always safe. If you downgrade, you'll retain access to your existing data but may lose access to certain features until you upgrade again.


Do you offer team or enterprise pricing?
We offer volume discounts for teams. Contact our sales team for custom pricing based on your team size and needs.

What reports are included in each plan?
Core includes Activity Reports for tracking your daily sales activities. Advanced adds Pipeline Report and Referrals Report. Premium unlocks All Reports including Login History and comprehensive analytics.

How does geocoding work across plans?
All plans include daily geocoding allowances: Core gets 20/day, Advanced gets 40/day, and Premium gets 60/day. Premium users can purchase additional geocodes at $0.10 each if they exceed their daily limit.

How does AI Lead Discovery work?
Our AI analyzes your existing prospect patterns and sales data to suggest high-quality leads that match your ideal customer profile. Available in the Premium plan with limited queries.
