 Pipeline Stages

A clear, consistent pipeline helps reps stay organized, forecast accurately, and move deals forward without losing momentum. These are the recommended ProProspector pipeline stages designed for field reps, small teams, and growing businesses.



 1. New Lead
A new contact added to the system. No outreach has been made yet.

Goal: Verify the lead is real and worth pursuing.  
Actions:  
 Confirm business details  
 Tag industry and lead source  
 Add initial notes



 2. Contacted
Initial outreach has been made (call, visit, email, or message).

Goal: Start a conversation.  
Actions:  
 Log outreach attempt  
 Add follow‑up reminder  
 Capture first interaction notes



 3. Engaged
The lead has responded or shown interest.

Goal: Understand their needs and qualify the opportunity.  
Actions:  
 Ask discovery questions  
 Identify pain points  
 Determine decision maker  
 Add qualification notes



 4. Qualified
The lead is a good fit and has a real need, budget, or timeline.

Goal: Move toward a proposal or demo.  
Actions:  
 Confirm requirements  
 Share relevant materials  
 Prepare next steps



 5. Proposal / Demo
A formal offer, quote, or product demonstration has been delivered.

Goal: Present value clearly and address objections.  
Actions:  
 Send proposal or pricing  
 Conduct demo or walkthrough  
 Log objections and responses  
 Set a decision date



 6. Negotiation
The lead is interested but needs adjustments, clarifications, or internal approval.

Goal: Reach a mutually beneficial agreement.  
Actions:  
 Adjust terms if needed  
 Provide additional documentation  
 Follow up consistently



 7. Closed Won
The deal is successfully closed.

Goal: Ensure smooth onboarding and maintain relationship.  
Actions:  
 Send welcome materials  
 Add onboarding tasks  
 Tag deal as “Won”



 8. Closed Lost
The deal did not close.

Goal: Capture insights for future improvement.  
Actions:  
 Log reason for loss  
 Tag competitor if applicable  
 Add notes for future re‑engagement



 9. Follow‑Up / Recycle
A lead that wasn’t ready but may be valuable later.

Goal: Keep the relationship warm.  
Actions:  
 Set long‑term reminders  
 Add nurture notes  
 Revisit periodically



A well‑structured pipeline helps reps stay focused, reduces confusion, and ensures no opportunity slips through the cracks.
